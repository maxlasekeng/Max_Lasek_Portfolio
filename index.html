A design is only as good as how it’s built and tested.

As Manipulation Team Lead for UB Space Bulls, I lead a team of more than 15 people designing and manufacturing a 5-DOF robotic arm for the University Rover Challenge. My own part of that arm is the end effector, and most of what I’ve learned from it came after the CAD was done: dialing in tolerances, tracking down friction and backlash, and making sure it bolts up to the wrist my teammates designed.

I joined the team as a new member and noticed that many people left after a meeting or two because there was no clear way in. I spent a summer rebuilding onboarding, and I now mentor the members who stayed.

Before UB I led a VEX Robotics team that won the Excellence Award and qualified for the World Championship. I’m an Eagle Scout, and outside the lab I lift weights and play guitar.
